A sector is a leaf (fires its command) or a branch (chevron on the outer edge, opens its children in place). The parent collapses to a breadcrumb arc inside the hub; tap the hub to step back, or tap it at the root to close. Node shape is { label, icon, command, children } — identical to a macro line.
